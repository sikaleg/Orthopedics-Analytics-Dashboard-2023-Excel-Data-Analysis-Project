# Orthopedics Analytics Dashboard 2023 (Excel)
## Project Overview
The Orthopedics Analytics Dashboard (2023) is an Excel-based healthcare data analysis project focused on evaluating patient volume, satisfaction scores, and wait times for the Orthopedics department using 2023 patient data. The project involved data cleaning, creation of time-based columns (Month, Day of the Week, Time Frame), and development of an interactive dashboard to generate actionable operational insights.
## Project Objective
To analyze 2023 patient data for the Orthopedics department in order to evaluate patient volume, satisfaction levels, and wait times, and to generate actionable insights that support performance monitoring and operational improvement using Microsoft Excel.
## Business Questions
- What was the overall performance of the Orthopedics department in 2023?
- How did patient satisfaction vary month by month?
- How many patients visited the Orthopedics department each month?
- Which age group required Orthopedic services the most?
- Does patient volume affect wait time?
- What days of the week experience higher patient traffic?
- During which time frame do we experience longer wait times?

## Key Insight
Analysis of Orthopedics referrals in 2023 revealed important patterns in patient volume, wait times, and satisfaction scores:
- January recorded the highest number of patient visits (65 out of 531 total patients), highlighting periods of increased workload that may require additional staffing or resource support.
- The dominant age group was 1–10 years old (73 patients), indicating a significant pediatric patient population that may benefit from specialized care and targeted resources.
- Patient satisfaction was lowest in June, with an average score of 4.1, suggesting potential service or operational challenges during that period.
- The highest average wait time recorded was 41 minutes, and analysis showed that periods with higher patient volume corresponded to longer wait times, emphasizing the importance of effective scheduling and workflow management.
- Patient traffic also varied by day of the week and time frame, presenting opportunities to optimize staff allocation and improve service efficiency during busier periods.

Overall, these insights provide actionable guidance for hospital administrators to enhance patient experience, allocate resources efficiently, and manage department workflow effectively.

## Conclusion
The Orthopedics Analysis successfully transformed raw patient data into actionable insights for hospital management. By analyzing Orthopedics referrals, this project highlighted trends in patient volume, wait times, satisfaction scores, and demographic distribution.

Key findings show that periods with higher patient visits corresponded to longer wait times and lower satisfaction scores, while pediatric patients (1–10 years) formed a significant portion of visits. These insights emphasize the need for optimized staffing, efficient scheduling, and targeted resource allocation to improve patient experience and operational efficiency.

Overall, this analysis demonstrates how data-driven decision-making can support the Orthopedics department in enhancing service delivery and patient care outcomes.
